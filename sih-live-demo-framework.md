# SIH Live Demo Framework — PARAM-BRAHMAND Pitch Deck

**Target:** Technical jury for ISRO Problem Statement PS 26167 (SatQuery AI / Smart India Hackathon 2026)

To win over a technical jury, your presentation cannot just be slides of equations—it needs to translate heavy radar physics into visual, instant proof points.

Here is a high-impact, step-by-step framework to showcase your Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) and AI capabilities live on the presentation platform:

## 1. The 30-Second Hook (The Real-World Crisis)

* **Start with a Failure:** Open your screen with a standard satellite or general-purpose AI model (like Google or OpenAI models) failing during a monsoon flood because thick clouds blind optical sensors.
* **The Pivot:** Instantly switch to your system, PARAM-BRAHMAND, demonstrating how microwave radar cuts through 100% cloud cover to reveal exact water levels underneath.

## 2. The Live UI "Aha!" Moment (Optical vs. SAR Toggle)

* **Split-Screen Toggle:** On your dashboard, show a dual-pane view:
  * **Left:** A cloudy optical image where nothing is visible.
  * **Right:** Your processed SAR image mapping flooded zones instantly.
* **Sub-Canopy Demonstration:** Zoom into a forested flood region. Show how your system uses the RVoG and Yamaguchi decomposition models to "see through" tree leaves and detect standing water trapped beneath dense jungle canopies.

## 3. Visualizing the Math Simply (Yamaguchi & De-biasing)

* **Avoid Wall-of-Equations:** When the jury asks about the backend math, don't read the formulas. Show a quick 3D animation or color-coded breakdown:
  * **Surface (P_s):** Highlight calm water and roads in blue.
  * **Double-Bounce (P_d):** Highlight buildings and bridge piers in red.
* **Show the Fix (θ_rot):** Demonstrate how rotating the city grid eliminates building tilt bias, dropping urban misclassification from 18.4% down to 1.1% right before their eyes.

## 4. Proving Zero Hallucination (The Dharma-Chakra Firewall)

* **The Trap:** Mention how standard AI models frequently hallucinate water on 30-degree mountain slopes.
* **The Proof:** Intentionally feed a bad prompt or trigger a physically impossible boundary condition. Show how your Dharma-Chakra physics firewall instantly catches the error, cross-checks radar backscatter (σ₀ > -10 dB), and rejects the false prediction in milliseconds.

## 5. The Grand Finale (Voice & Multi-Lingual Speed)

* **Hands-Free Query:** Use the built-in voice accessibility feature. Speak a command in Hindi or English ("Show me flood damage in Assam").
* **The Metric Win:** Flash your key performance indicators on screen: **97.2% flood mapping accuracy under 100% cloud cover** rendered in just **380 ms**.
